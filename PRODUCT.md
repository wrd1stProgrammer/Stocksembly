# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- Global self-directed investors researching US-listed companies.
- Korean and English bilingual investors who need issuer-grade context without losing comprehension.
- Time-constrained professionals who want an auditable research file instead of an AI answer stream.

## Product Purpose

Stocksembly turns one US ticker into a versioned Research File. Four specialist departments investigate independently, challenge one another, link evidence, and submit their work to an independent Research Chair. The product explains the state of the company and the expectations embedded in its price; it does not issue BUY/SELL labels, entry prices, or target prices.

## Brand Personality

Investigative, composed, transparent.

## Anti-references

- Generic trading dashboards dominated by price alerts and flashing market color.
- Casino-like crypto pages, neon speculation, or guaranteed-return language.
- AI chat wrappers that hide sources and compress disagreement into one confident answer.
- Game interfaces that make evidence collection feel trivial or unserious.
- Prediction and target-price sales pages.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence before prediction.
2. Show the research organization, not a loading spinner.
3. Preserve disagreement and make resolution criteria explicit.
4. Keep one dominant action: start with a ticker.
5. Prefer real product truth and auditable artifacts over decorative theatre.
6. Maintain equal English and Korean information density.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA, including keyboard-first operation and visible focus.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences without removing equivalent state information.
- Never communicate status by color alone.
- Keep Korean and English layouts semantically equivalent with natural CJK line breaking.
